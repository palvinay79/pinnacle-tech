Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Pinnacle Tech collects information necessary to provide website hosting, technical maintenance, and ongoing development services. When you create a client portal account or request support, we collect contact details (such as name, email address, phone number) and website domain details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to maintain your cloud web hosting account, process subscription billing through our secure client portal, communicate regarding maintenance schedules, and fulfill technical support tickets.

3. Data Protection & Security

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS) for data transmission. Account credentials, server access keys, and client records are stored in secure environments with restricted access.

4. Third-Party Billing & Client Portal

Subscription orders and payment transactions are processed securely through our client portal and integrated merchant gateways. Pinnacle Tech does not store full credit card details on our marketing website.

5. Contacting Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email us at support@pinnacletech.us.
