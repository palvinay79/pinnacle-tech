How Pinnacle Tech Works

What happens after I pay?

We design, host, maintain and continuously improve your website — for one predictable monthly price. Here is our exact step-by-step process:

01
STEP ONE

Create Your Account

Create your secure client account and select your plan.

BASIC ($40/mo)

Hosting, SSL, backups, maintenance & basic support.

ADVANCED ($60/mo) ★ Popular

Faster support, extra dev hours & speed optimization.

PRO ($80/mo)

Priority dev turnaround & advanced performance.

02
STEP TWO

Tell Us About Your Business

Tell us about your business through our ticket management in the client portal: send your logo, business information, images, and website requirements.

Private Client Portal: Our ticket management system lets you easily attach files, share brand colors, outline desired pages, and track progress at any time.
03
STEP THREE

Choose Your Design

Select from our available website templates. Pick the modern layout and look that fits your industry and brand identity, and we will customize it to your exact specifications.

04
STEP FOUR

We Build / Setup Your Website

Our team configures your website and hosting within 24 hours. We set up high-speed cloud infrastructure, SSL security, staging environments, and optimize all initial assets.

24-Hour Policy: Speed and responsiveness are core to our standard. We configure and set up your initial site environment within 24 hours.
05
STEP FIVE

Website Goes Live

Your domain is connected and your website becomes live. We verify DNS records, test cross-browser responsiveness, check mobile viewports, and launch with zero downtime.

06
STEP SIX

Request Changes Anytime

Submit website updates through your client portal. Need new text, photos, a new promotional section, or seasonal changes? Just open a ticket in your dashboard and our technical team takes care of it promptly.

Ready to Start?

Select your package and let Pinnacle Tech handle your website technical operations.
