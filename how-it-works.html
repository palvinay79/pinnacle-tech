Seamless Onboarding

How Pinnacle Tech Works

A simple, hassle-free process designed to get your website supported and maintained in minutes.

01
STEP ONE

Choose Your Plan

Select the monthly package that best matches your support and development needs:

BASIC ($40/mo)

Hosting, SSL, backups, maintenance & basic support.

ADVANCED ($60/mo) ★ Most Popular

Faster support, extra dev hours & speed optimization.

PRO ($80/mo)

Priority dev turnaround & advanced performance.

02
STEP TWO

Create Your WHMCS Account

Clicking "Get Started" redirects you directly to our client portal powered by WHMCS. You will create your secure customer account, select your billing preference, and complete subscription activation.

Secure WHMCS Portal: All recurring subscription orders, invoices, and support tickets are managed securely inside WHMCS.
03
STEP THREE

Tell Us About Your Business

Once your order is confirmed, you will receive an onboarding questionnaire where you can share your existing website URL or domain preferences. If migrating an existing website, our team handles the transfer seamlessly with zero downtime.

04
STEP FOUR

We Manage & Support Your Website

Your website is live, fast, and fully monitored. Whenever you need a text update, new image uploaded, or technical maintenance task done, simply submit a request through your WHMCS portal and our technical team handles it!

Ready to Start?

Select your package and let Pinnacle Tech handle your website technical operations.
