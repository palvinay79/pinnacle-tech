How Pinnacle Tech Works

We design, host, maintain and continuously improve your website — for one predictable monthly price. Getting started takes less than 3 minutes.

01
STEP ONE

Choose Your Plan

Select the monthly package that best matches your support and development needs:

BASIC ($40/mo)

Hosting, SSL, backups, maintenance & basic support.

ADVANCED ($60/mo) ★ Most Popular

Faster support, extra dev hours & speed optimization.

PRO ($80/mo)

Priority dev turnaround & advanced performance.

02
STEP TWO

Create Your Client Portal Account

Clicking "Get Started" redirects you directly to our secure client portal. You will create your account, select your billing preference, and complete subscription activation in just a few minutes.

Secure Client Portal: All recurring subscription orders, invoices, and support tickets are managed securely inside your private dashboard.
03
STEP THREE

Select a Demo Template & Submit Your Ticket

Browse our wide collection of modern demo templates to find the design that matches your brand. Then, simply log into your client dashboard to submit a ticket with your chosen template, website domain, and business details. Our technical team configures and tailors everything for you.

04
STEP FOUR

We Manage, Support & Continuously Improve

Your website is live, fast, and fully monitored. Whenever you need new content, design revisions, image updates, or speed optimizations, simply open a ticket in your client dashboard. It is our strict policy to solve tickets and reply within 24 hours.
